**Ask and confirm**

Name who the change is for and what it must let them do. List the open points that are business-shaped (behavior a customer sees, failure and recovery, what to leave out) and ask the person about them, decisions first, in one batch. Turn the answers into requirements with stable IDs R1, R2, ... that are never renumbered, plus non-goals. Play them back once and confirm. Technical questions are not asked: decide them in the plan.
