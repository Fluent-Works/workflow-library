Start from the promise, not the diff: the ticket, the functional outcome in scope.md and the states in design.md when they exist. Prove each one by running something (a test, the app, a script, a request), and record what you ran and what you saw. A requirement you could not exercise is reported as unverified, never as passing.

Probe every user-facing step with seven questions: nothing (empty input or no data), many (large volumes), wrong (invalid input), broken (a dependency fails), again (repeated or concurrent), back (undo, navigate away and return) and someone else (another user or permission level).

When the change touches the user interface, prove it in the running product: launch the app the way the project documents, walk the changed feature and register screenshots. What only a person can do goes in your summary as exact steps.
