Separate the problem from the proposed solution, and user value from feature requests. Sort needs into must, should and could.

For any choice with more than one viable option, ask rather than guess. Decide silently only when exactly one option is viable, and say so in one line. Batch related questions. Open each question with one line of situation, put your recommended option first, give two to four options, and state what each option commits the work to. Write for a reader without your context: gloss domain terms, name repo concepts with their path, cut insider words. If the person stays vague, offer to proceed with what you have or stop; do not grind.

Never invent facts about users, the market or the system; mark what you assume as an assumption.
